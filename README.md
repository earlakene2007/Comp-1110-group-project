# Monthly Budget Tracker
#### Repository: https://github.com/earlakene2007/Comp-1110-group-project.git
## Description:
Users will enter their income, set a monthly limit, and record expenses under different categories. The program will calculate total monthly income, total expenses, and the remaining balance. It will also show spending by category and tell users whether they have exceeded their spending limit.
## Functions:
- add_income(): adds income to the monthly budget, ie what the program will compare the total monthly expenses to.
- calculate_total_income(): calculates the sum of the recorded income.
- add_expense(): adds the expense with its category.
- calculate_total_expense: calculates the sum of all the monthly expenses.
- calculate_balance(): calculates the total income minus the total expenses.
- check_budget(): compares the final balance against the monthly budget.
